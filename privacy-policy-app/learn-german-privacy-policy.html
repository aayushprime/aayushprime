Privacy Policy

Deutsch lernen · Last updated October 14, 2026

Deutsch lernen is an app for learning German through short readings, quizzes, flashcards and grammar lessons. This policy explains what data the iOS and Android apps handle.

No account, nothing we collect

The app has no sign-up and no account. Your progress, stars, saved flashcards, streak and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run analytics and we do not receive or store any personal data. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising

Lessons are unlocked by watching a short video ad served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Information about ad interactions and app diagnostics

On iOS, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking when the app asks; otherwise Google serves non-personalized ads. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Google’s use of this data is described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Updates

The app downloads new lessons and improvements from our update server. These requests include the app version and platform, and no personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
