Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Carrom Strike app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Aayush Lamichhane (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free, ad-supported service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


Information Collection and Use

When you first open the Application it creates a guest account for you on the Service Provider's game server. To run that account, online matches and leaderboards, the Application stores the following on the server:

  • A random identifier generated by the Application on your device (used to sign you back in as the same guest)
  • Your in-game profile: display name, chosen avatar, frame and striker, level, experience, coins, gems, rating, wins and losses, and the items you have unlocked
  • Results of online matches (opponent, winner, rating change and the shots played) and daily gift / rewarded-ad claim counts
  • Your device's Internet Protocol address, as part of normal server logs and rate limiting

Your display name, avatar, frame, level and rating are visible to other players in matches and on the leaderboards.

The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device and does not ask for your contacts, photos or microphone.


Sign in with Apple / Google (optional)

You may link your guest account to Sign in with Apple or Google so you can restore your progress on another device. When you do, the Service Provider receives and stores only the opaque account identifier issued by Apple or Google for this Application. Your email address and name from Apple or Google are not requested or stored.


Advertising

The Application shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads that grant in-game coins. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), only if you allow it
  • Your device's Internet Protocol address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system version and app version
  • Interactions with ads (for example, ads viewed or tapped)

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, the Application asks for your permission before your device's advertising identifier can be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized, and the advertising identifier is not shared. You can change this choice at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Data Retention and Deletion

The Service Provider keeps your account data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time inside the Application: Home > Settings (gear) > Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, coins, gems, items and rank and unlinks any Apple or Google sign-in. If you cannot open the Application, see how to request deletion or email lamichhaneaayush5@gmail.com. Anonymous match statistics with no name or identifier may be kept for game balancing.


Children

The Application is not directed to children under the age of 13, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact the Service Provider (lamichhaneaayush5@gmail.com) so the account can be deleted.


Security

Data between the Application and the game server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS / secure WebSocket). The Service Provider provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect the information it processes and maintains.
