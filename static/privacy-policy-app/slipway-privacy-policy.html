Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Slipway: Arrow Block Puzzle app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Aayush Lamichhane (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free, ad-supported service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


Information Collection and Use

The Application does not require an account and does not ask you for your name, email address or any other personal details.

Your game progress (levels, coins, themes and settings) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to the Service Provider.

The Application shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), only if you allow it
  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address) and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system version and app version
  • Interactions with ads (for example, ads viewed or tapped)


The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device.


App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, the Application asks for your permission before your device's advertising identifier can be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized, and the advertising identifier is not shared. You can change this choice at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Opt-Out Rights

You can limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; Android: Settings > Google > Ads). You can stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling it.
