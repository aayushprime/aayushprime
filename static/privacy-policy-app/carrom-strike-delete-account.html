Delete your Carrom Strike account

Carrom Strike is developed by Aayush Lamichhane.


In the app (immediate)
  1. Open Carrom Strike.
  2. On the Home screen, tap the gear (Settings).
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.


Without the app

Email lamichhaneaayush5@gmail.com with the subject "Carrom Strike account deletion" and your in-game name. The account is deleted within 30 days.


What is deleted
  • Your profile: display name, avatar, frame, striker, level, experience, rating, wins and losses
  • Your coins, gems and unlocked items
  • Your leaderboard entries
  • Linked Sign in with Apple / Google identifiers and your device's guest identifier

What may be kept: anonymous match statistics with no name or identifier, used for game balancing, and server logs, which are rotated within 30 days.


See also the Carrom Strike Privacy Policy.
